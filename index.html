◂◂Who We Are▸▸

Where expertise meets dedication

We are a Kenyan corporate and commercial law firm guiding individuals and businesses through today's complex legal and regulatory landscape. Our litigation practice covers corporate, criminal and civil litigation, alternative dispute resolution, estate and trusts, immigration, labour & employment, conveyancing and real estate.

We advise investors and investees across agribusiness, banking, energy, financial services, fintech, insurance, manufacturing, and telecommunications — in Kenya and across the region. We take pride in dynamic lawyering that is prompt, proactive, and result-oriented, delivered with a professional, collaborative work ethic.
