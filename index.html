About Us

Home/About Us

Where Expertise Meets Dedication

A rapidly growing, full-service Kenyan law firm.

Right by your side always

Ong'ato, Ochieng' & Co. Advocates was founded in 2020 and practises from Kims House on Kijabe Street in Nairobi. Your matter is handled by a partner from the first consultation through to its conclusion. Our partners and consultants come from different parts of Kenya and bring a professional, collaborative way of working to every matter.

In the years since, we have acted in more than 300 matters. Some were commercial agreements that never saw a courtroom. Others were disputes that ran their full course. The work is deliberately broad because our clients' problems rarely arrive neatly labelled, and a firm that only litigates tends to advise litigation.

Who We Act For

Individuals, families, property owners and businesses: a couple checking title before they buy, a company drafting its shareholders' agreement, an employee dismissed without notice, a family dividing an estate. Where a matter falls outside what we do well, we say so and help you find someone who does.

Property Agency

We connect vendors with serious purchasers of land, homes and vehicles, and our advocates verify ownership, authority to sell and title before any listing goes live, and our partners have worked with leading developers in Nairobi, including on the development and sale of 306 apartments in Westlands and 32 executive three-bedroom apartments along Langata Road.

Four Commitments

01

We answer

Calls and messages are returned, and we tell you when we cannot give an answer yet.

02

We put it in writing

Advice, terms and costs come to you in writing, in language you can act on.

03

We say no

When a claim is weak or a settlement beats the likely judgment, we tell you plainly.

04

We keep it confidential

Everything you tell us is confidential, including if you decide not to instruct us.

From First Call to Conclusion

  1. 1

    First contact

    Call, WhatsApp or use the contact form. We ask what has happened and what you would like the outcome to be, then tell you what to bring to a consultation.

  2. 2

    Written advice and terms

    After the meeting you receive our advice in writing, with our terms of engagement and how costs are calculated. Nothing substantive proceeds until you agree them.

  3. 3

    Kept informed

    While the matter is live we report on what has moved and what has not. You always know who is handling your file.

Our Standards
Confidential

From the first consultation, whether or not you instruct us.

Client account

Client funds are held separately from the firm’s money and accounted for.

Answerable

We practise under the professional conduct rules for advocates in Kenya.

Tell us briefly what has happened and what you are hoping to achieve. We will tell you whether we can help, what the next step is, and what it is likely to involve.
